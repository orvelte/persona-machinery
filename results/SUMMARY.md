**What the work supports.** In Qwen3.5-9B the Assistant Axis is a stable direction already present in the base model, confirming Lu et al. for this family. Its clearest pretraining correlate is *voice*: on raw passages it separates impersonal third-person reference text from any character speaking in its own voice, and it is not a helpful-character direction. This is a projection result on raw passages. It sits alongside, not against, Lu et al.'s base-model steering result, where pushing along the axis elicits helpful archetypes: what the axis reads in text and what pushing along it produces need not be the same thing. The axis is written diffusely, mostly by late-layer components, and it does not depend on the chat template's role markers. Under a context perturbation, RL deepens the Assistant basin relative to DPO, but the stages do not deepen it monotonically. Its causal role in behaviour, the narrative-convention explanation of the judge distinction, and the persona-vector relationship remain open.

**Limitations.** The raw-document gaming floor could not be lifted (Q2b). No persona vector steered at usable coherence (Q5). Q6's same-layer kick could not test basin depth; Q6b replaces it. The Q3 ablation had no efficacy control.

**Next steps the results point to.**
- **A causal test of the AA:** an edit that includes prompt positions, or adds a role displacement, with an efficacy control on a behaviour that has headroom.
- **A basin test:** steer until off-persona text is in the context, then read out above L\*.
- **Trait steering:** a grid between c = 1 and c = 2, and a coherence judge that does not penalise the trait itself.
