**What the work supports.** In Qwen3.5-9B the Assistant Axis is a stable direction already present in the base model. It is written by late-layer components in roughly equal measure, rather than by one circuit. On raw text it specifically marks impersonal reference register, not helpful characters in stories. It does not depend on the chat template's role markers. Its causal role in behaviour, the basin question, and the persona-vector relationship remain open, because the interventions here did not pass their positive controls.

**Next steps the results point to.**
- **A causal test of the AA:** an edit that includes prompt positions, or adds a role displacement, with an efficacy control on a behaviour that has headroom.
- **A basin test:** steer until off-persona text is in the context, then read out above L\*.
- **Trait steering:** a grid between c = 1 and c = 2, and a coherence judge that does not penalise the trait itself.
